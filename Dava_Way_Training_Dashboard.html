Important reminders

• Fuel long runs over 90 minutes; gradually practise 60–90 g carbohydrate/hour if tolerated.
• Do not force mileage through pain or poor recovery. Repeat or reduce a week when needed.
• Week 40 should align with the confirmed 2027 race date. The organiser's date needs checking.
• Sub-5 requires about 6:00/km for 50 km or 5:53/km for 51 km, before accounting for course conditions.
